Executive Sales Performance Dashboard
Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Executive Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. it provides insights into sales performance, profitability, customer activity, and regional performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.
Objectives
Monitor key sales and business performance indicator 
Analyze sales and profit across product categories and regions 
Understand customer and sales segment performance 
Identify trends that can support business decisions.
Tools and Technologies
Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions),Sample Super Dataset
Dashboard Features
Total Sales,Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Customers, Average Order Value, Profit Margin, Monthly Sales Trend, Sales by Category and Region, profit by Category, Top 10 Customer by Sales, Sales by Shipping Mode and Segment, Interactive Year and Region Filters.
Key Business Insights
The dashboard highlights sales trends, profitable product categories, regional performance, customer contribution, and shipping patterns to support business analysis and decision-making.
